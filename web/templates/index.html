CONTROL GUIDE

Virtual Mouse

Control the Windows pointer using hand landmarks detected by the camera. Start the engine from Dashboard and keep your hand clearly visible in the camera view.

☝️

Move Cursor

Raise your index finger and move it inside the camera view to move the pointer.

🤏

Left Click

Bring your thumb and index finger together to perform a left click.

🤏

Right Click

Bring your thumb and middle finger together to perform a right click.

🖐️

Drag

Use the open-hand gesture shown in the dashboard's Normal Mode guide to drag items. Keep the gesture steady while moving.

✊

Scroll

Use the fist gesture shown in the Normal Mode guide; move your hand as instructed by the live gesture feedback.

👌

Double Click

Bring your thumb and index finger together in the configured double-click gesture, as shown in the dashboard guide.

Tips
  • Use good lighting and keep the full hand inside the camera frame.
  • Start with Dashboard → Start Virtual Mouse, then select Browser Vision or Separate Window.
  • Use Stop Engine on Dashboard when finished.

GESTURE SHORTCUTS

Media Mode

Control supported media playback actions with thumb-to-finger gestures after Media Mode is activated.

01

Activate Media Mode

Hold the pinky-up pose with index, middle, and ring fingers down until the mode activates.

02

Play / Pause

Bring your thumb and index finger together.

03

Next Track

Bring your thumb and middle finger together.

04

Previous Track

Bring your thumb and ring finger together.

05

Exit Media Mode

Release the activation pose first, then show an open palm to exit.

Tip

Keep gestures distinct and briefly release between actions to avoid accidental repeated commands.

SYSTEM AUDIO

Volume Mode

Adjust system volume by changing the distance between your thumb and index finger.

01

Activate

Bring thumb and index finger together, with middle, ring, and pinky fingers down, and hold until Volume Mode activates.

02

Adjust Volume

Keep index and middle fingers raised while ring and pinky stay down. Change the thumb-to-index distance to adjust volume; the value is mapped and smoothed by the engine.

03

Return to Normal Mode

Move out of the volume pose. The engine returns to Normal Mode after its configured exit delay.

Tip

Keep your hand inside the camera frame and make gradual distance changes for smoother control.

DISPLAY CONTROL

Brightness Mode

Adjust display brightness using the thumb-to-index distance while holding the configured three-finger pose.

01

Activate

Bring thumb and index finger together and hold until Brightness Mode activates.

02

Adjust Brightness

Raise index, middle, and ring fingers while keeping the pinky down. Change the distance between thumb and index finger to adjust brightness.

03

Exit

Show an open palm to return to Normal Mode, or move out of the brightness pose and wait for the configured exit delay.

Compatibility

Brightness control depends on the computer/display and the brightness controller supported by the project.

OFFLINE SPEECH RECOGNITION

Voice Mode

The project uses Vosk with SpeechRecognition for offline English command recognition. Voice commands require a working microphone and the local Vosk model.

Supported voice phrases

How to use
  • Make sure the microphone is connected and allowed by Windows.
  • Ensure the Vosk model folder exists at models/vosk-model-small-en-us-0.15.
  • Speak one supported phrase clearly in English. Recognition runs locally, but commands are acted on only when the voice listener is running and the command callback is connected.
